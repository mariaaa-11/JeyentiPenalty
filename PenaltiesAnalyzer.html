📜 Penalty Rules

When Plan Travel Time (PTT) > 12 hours

  • Delayed Time (DT) from 1 minute to 2 hours → fixed penalty ₱3,000
  • Delayed Time (DT) > 2 hours → fixed penalty ₱5,000

When Plan Travel Time (PTT) is 0 to 12 hours

  • DT from 1 minute to 30 minutes → 2% of the trip rate
  • DT > 30 minutes → 5% of the trip rate

Rate lookup basis

  • Primary: SLU 3PL VisMin table matched by Route + Supplier
  • Fallback: SLU 3PL Local Trips matched by DC / Branch codes
  • Multi-drop example 490-520-521 → uses first & last leg → 490-521
  • Reverse / return example 520-490 → uses Backload rate
  • You may override the rate manually on any calculation
