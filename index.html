Penalty Rules

PTT > 12 hours

  • DT 1 min – 2 hours → ₱3,000
  • DT > 2 hours → ₱5,000

PTT 0 – 12 hours

  • DT 1 min – 30 min → 2% of rate
  • DT > 30 min → 5% of rate

STATUS

  • Disputed → no penalty (₱0)
  • Broken → 20% of rate
  • Penalty → travel-time rules above

Rate basis

  • Route label: first leg → second-to-last leg
  • If second-to-last is 490 → backload
  • Matched by route + trucker (fuzzy)
